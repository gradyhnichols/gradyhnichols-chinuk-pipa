The historical texts the data is derived from were published between 1863 and 1924 (see
`data/lexicon/sources.yaml` and `data/gt/README.md`), so they are in the public domain in the United States.
Short quotations from modern works keep their authors' copyright and are cited as quotations. Unicode character
names and annotations quoted from the Unicode Standard are © Unicode, Inc.

The sign data also records analysis and transcriptions by modern authors, chiefly David D. Robertson
(his 2011 dissertation and his blog posts) and the Kaltash Wawa *Chinuk Pipa Guide*. That work remains theirs. It
is cited where it is used, and this licence covers only this project's compilation and annotations, not their
analysis. If you are one of these authors and want an entry changed or removed, please open an issue or contact the
maintainer through GitHub.

Code files inside `data/` (for example `data/signs/*.py`) are licensed Apache-2.0 like the rest of the code.

Lexicon glosses are quoted as printed in 1863–1924 and include terms now considered offensive. They are kept as
historical evidence, not endorsed.
